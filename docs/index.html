eMudhra Impact Tracker

  • G3 impact list — endpoints on the 8 compromised intermediates (dies with the 2026-10-27 Chrome removal). None found.
  • G1 impact list — endpoints on eMudhra's G1 family (still trusted today; reissue on a planned timeline). 247 domains / 533 endpoints.
⏰ 2026-10-27: Chrome removes emSign ECC Root CA - G3 (root store v43). Microsoft has blocked the 8 compromised intermediates since 2026-10-20; Mozilla removed the G3 root from NSS the same day. Legacy emSign Root CA - G1 is not removed yet — Chrome is evaluating it, and eMudhra is migrating affected customers onto it.

G3 impact list — compromised intermediates

These chains break immediately: Microsoft blocks the intermediates since 2026-10-20, Mozilla NSS dropped emSign ECC Root CA - G3 the same day, and Chrome removes the root on 2026-10-27. The Sectigo AAA cross-sign was revoked 2026-10-19, so there is no fallback path.

✅ 0 endpoints found across all 10,808 scanned domains. No Indian gov site is on the compromised intermediates, so the 2026-10-27 Chrome removal does not hard-break any scanned gov site. The checked-for intermediates:
EM DV TLS CA - G3B · EM OV TLS CA - G3B · EM EV TLS CA - G3B · EM DV TLS CA - G3C · EM OV TLS CA - G3C · EM EV TLS CA - G3C (+ EM OV CS CA - G3B/G3C, code-signing)

G1 impact list — emSign G1 family

247 domains serving certificates that terminate at emSign Root CA - G1 (legacy root, present in all major stores) or at emSign Root TLS CA - G1 (2024, Microsoft-only root cross-signed by legacy G1). All these sites work today. They break if Chrome/Mozilla remove legacy emSign Root CA - G1 (under evaluation) — and eMudhra is exiting the public TLS business regardless, so reissue from another CA is the recommended action, planned rather than emergency.

Methodology

  • Universe: registry dump of gov.in/nic.in domains, expanded with (a) a full IGOD (india.gov.in web-directory) dump across all 36 states/UTs and (b) a link crawl over every TLS-reachable host's homepage, robots.txt and sitemap.xml harvesting cross-referenced gov domains; per domain the apex and www. were probed on :443 (fallback :8443), plus a common-prefix vhost probe (portal/services/sso/apps/…) over impacted domains.
  • Chains captured with openssl s_client -showcerts; a host is flagged when its leaf issuer or any presented chain element matches the compromised intermediates or carries eMudhra/emSign branding.
  • G1 paths: emSign SSL CA - G1 leaves chain directly to legacy emSign Root CA - G1; EM * TLS CA - G2A-1 leaves chain to the Microsoft-only emSign Root TLS CA - G1, cross-signed by legacy G1.
  • Limitations: HTTPS on 443/8443 only; full CT-based vhost enumeration is pending (crt.sh rate limits); universe is the registry snapshot, not a live registry.

If you operate one of these sites

  1. Check your chain: openssl s_client -connect yourhost:443 -servername yourhost -showcerts | openssl x509 -noout -issuer.
  2. Reissue from a CA outside the eMudhra/emSign family and redeploy. Update clients too (ca-certificates, certifi ≥ 2026.10.19, Go ≥ 1.27.3, Node ≥ 22.23.4; rebuild containers older than 2026-10-20).
  3. Indian gov sites on CCA-India chains (NIC sub-CAs) are a separate matter — those roots are not in public trust stores and are unaffected by this incident.
